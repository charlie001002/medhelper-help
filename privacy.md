## 1. Who we are

MedHelper ("the app") is developed and maintained by **Charlie Scott** ("I", "me"). If you have any questions about this policy, please contact me at: [charliescott61@icloud.com](mailto:charliescott61@icloud.com)

---

## 2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data MedHelper collects, how it is stored, and your rights in relation to that data. It applies to all versions of MedHelper on iOS.

---

## 3. Data I collect and why

### 3.1 Data you provide

| Data | Purpose | Stored where |
|---|---|---|
| Your name | Personalise the app display | On your device only |
| Medication names, doses, schedules | Core app function — tracking your prescriptions | On your device only |
| Dose history (taken / skipped / missed) | Adherence tracking and reports | On your device only |
| Stock levels | Low-stock alerts and reorder reminders | On your device only |
| App settings (mode, notification preferences) | App configuration | On your device only |

### 3.2 Data fetched from external sources

When you search for a medication by name, MedHelper queries the **NHS Dictionary of Medicines and Devices (dm+d)** to provide name suggestions. The search term you type is sent to that service. No other information about you or your prescriptions is included in this request.

I do not operate this service. Its use is subject to NHS Digital's own terms.

---

## 4. Data I do not collect

- I do not collect analytics, crash reports, or usage statistics.
- I do not create user accounts or require you to register.
- I do not use advertising identifiers or tracking cookies.
- I do not share your data with third parties for any purpose.
- I have no access to any data stored on your device.

---

## 5. Where your data is stored

All personal data — your name, medication details, dose history, and settings — is stored **exclusively on your device** using Apple's standard on-device storage (SQLite and system preferences). It is not uploaded to any server.

If you use Apple iCloud Backup, your device data (including MedHelper's database) may be included in your iCloud backup. This is controlled entirely by your iOS settings and is subject to [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

---

## 6. Notifications

If you use Real-time mode, MedHelper sends local notifications to your device to remind you to take medication. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not routed through any external server.

---

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device for as long as you use the app. To delete your data:

- **Delete individual prescriptions** from within the app at any time.
- **Delete all data** by uninstalling the app. This permanently removes the local database and all settings.

I hold no copy of your data and cannot delete it on your behalf.

---

## 8. Children

MedHelper is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has entered personal data into the app, you can remove it by editing or deleting the relevant prescriptions, or by uninstalling the app.

---

## 9. Your rights under UK GDPR

Because all data is stored locally on your device and I have no access to it, many standard GDPR rights (access, portability, erasure by request) are exercised directly through the app or by uninstalling it.

If you have questions about how your data is handled, please contact me at [charliescott61@icloud.com](mailto:charliescott61@icloud.com).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)**: [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk)

---

## 10. Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS's built-in security — device passcode, Face ID / Touch ID, and file-level encryption. I recommend keeping your device software up to date.

---

## 11. Medical disclaimer

MedHelper is an organisational aid only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always follow the guidance of your doctor, pharmacist, or healthcare professional.

---

## 12. Changes to this policy

If I make material changes to this policy, I will update the "Last updated" date above and provide notice within the app where reasonably practicable.
